ug Report: Privacy Policy & Compliance Tool Issues

There is an issue with the Compliance Checker and the Privacy Policy evaluation.  
The tool reports that the policy is complete with no missing elements, while several critical sections are actually missing.  
Below are the errors that should have been detected by the compliance system:

1) Missing specification of the types of personal data collected
   - The policy does not clearly define what categories of personal data are collected 
     (e.g., identity data, contact information, technical data, browsing data, cookies, etc.).
   - This is a mandatory component in any compliant privacy policy and should be flagged as incomplete.

2) Missing explanation of Data Subject Rights
   - The policy does not outline the user’s legal rights, such as:
     • Right of access  
     • Right to rectification  
     • Right to deletion (erasure)  
     • Right to object to processing  
     • Right to data portability  
     • How to submit complaints or contact the Data Protection Officer  
   - These rights are essential under data protection regulations and must be included.  
     The compliance checker should identify their absence.

3) Use of personal data for marketing without obtaining consent
   - The policy states that personal data may be used for marketing, offers, or promotional messages.  
   - However, it does NOT mention obtaining prior explicit user consent (opt-in) before using personal data for marketing purposes.  
   - This is a compliance violation, and the tool should have flagged it.

Summary:
The compliance checker is currently giving an inaccurate evaluation by marking the policy as complete, even though it is missing:
- A clear list of personal data types collected  
- A full explanation of data subject rights  
- A consent requirement for marketing activities

Please review and update the compliance detection logic so that these missing elements are properly flagged as errors in the privacy policy.
